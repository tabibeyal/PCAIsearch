# PCAIsearch — Project Identity

PCAIsearch is an AI-powered search engine for the Pali Canon (Pāḷi Tipiṭaka). Its purpose: help practitioners and scholars find Buddhist teachings through natural language questions, grounded in actual suttas.

**Core principle:** accuracy over creativity. Every answer must cite real passages from retrieved suttas. Hallucinating sutta numbers or paraphrasing source text is never acceptable.

**Scope:** Covers DN, MN, SN, AN, and Khuddaka Nikāya selections. Source: SuttaCentral dumps at `data/dumps/`.

**Users:** Range from lay practitioners ("what did the Buddha say about anger?") to scholars comparing teachings across nikāyas.
